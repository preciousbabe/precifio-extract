Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Zero Data Retention: Documents and extracted data are held only for the duration of your active session and are permanently deleted once you export your results or close the application.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information: Email address and authentication credentials when you register.
  • Usage data: Credit balance, number of extractions, and feature usage for billing and service improvement.
  • Uploaded documents: The files you upload for extraction (invoices, receipts, statements, etc.).
  • Export preferences: Your chosen export formats and connected integrations (e.g., Xero, QuickBooks).

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide, maintain, and improve the document extraction service.
  • To process payments and manage your credit balance.
  • To authenticate you and secure your account.
  • To communicate service updates, billing issues, or security alerts.

3. Data Retention & Deletion

We do not retain your uploaded documents or extracted data beyond your active session. Once you export or close the app, all associated files and structured outputs are permanently erased from our servers. We keep only anonymized usage metrics (e.g., extraction count) for capacity planning.

4. Third-Party Services

We integrate with third-party accounting platforms (Xero, QuickBooks) and payment processors. When you export data to these services, their privacy policies apply to data handled on their platforms. We do not share your documents with any other third parties for advertising or profiling.

5. Security

We use industry-standard encryption (TLS/SSL) for data in transit and secure storage for data at rest during processing. Access to production systems is restricted and logged. Despite these measures, no online service can guarantee absolute security.

6. Your Rights

  • Access & deletion: You may request a copy of your account data or deletion of your account at any time by contacting support.
  • Data portability: You can export your extracted data in JSON, Excel, or PDF format at any time.
  • Withdraw consent: You may disconnect integrations and stop using the service at any time.

7. Cookies & Tracking

We use essential cookies for authentication and session management only. We do not use tracking cookies for advertising or behavioral profiling.

8. Children's Privacy

Our service is not intended for users under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. We will notify users of material changes via email or in-app notice.
