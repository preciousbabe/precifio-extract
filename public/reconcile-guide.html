📖 What Is Reconciliation?

Reconciliation compares two sets of documents — Side A and Side B — and automatically finds matches between them.

Think of it like pairing socks from two laundry baskets. You don't need to know the brand of each sock beforehand. You just look at color, size, and pattern. Precifio Reconcile does the same thing with your documents — it looks at the data inside them and figures out which ones belong together.

Invoice #001 Invoice #002 Invoice #003
↓ ↓ ↓
PRECIFIO RECONCILE
↓ ↓ ↓
PO #A12 Bank Txn #45 Receipt #09

Side A could be invoices. Side B could be purchase orders. Or bank statements. Or delivery notes. Or lab results. Any document type works.

🚀 How to Use It — Step by Step

1

Prepare Your Documents (Two Ways)

Option A — From Extraction: Upload and extract your primary documents (Side A) using Precifio Extract as you normally would. PDFs, images, scans — anything works. Then click 🔄 Reconcile from the Export dropdown.

Option B — Direct Upload: Click the 🔄 Reconcile Documents button on your dashboard. Create a workspace, then upload CSV, Excel, or JSON files directly to Side A and Side B. No extraction needed.

2

Click "Reconcile"

After extraction, click the 🔄 Reconcile button in the Export dropdown. If this is your first time, you'll see a consent screen explaining that we need to store your structured data to perform matching.

3

Create or Choose a Workspace

A workspace is like a folder for one reconciliation job. You can create a new one (e.g., "August 2026 Reconciliation") or add to an existing workspace.

4

Add Comparison Data to Side B

Upload your comparison data. This can be:

  • A CSV, Excel, or JSON file (e.g., bank statement export, payment report, API dump)
  • More extracted documents from your Precifio queue

Each row in your CSV becomes a document on Side B. Column headers become field names.

5

Review Match Rules (Optional)

Precifio automatically detects which fields on Side A correspond to fields on Side B. Click ⚙ Match Rules to review or adjust these mappings. You can add, remove, or change how fields are compared.

6

Run Reconciliation

Click ▶ Run Reconciliation. Precifio compares every document on Side A against every document on Side B and assigns a match score.

7

Review Results

Results are grouped into four tabs: Matched, Partial, Review, and Unmatched. Click any tab to see the pairs and their scores.

Click 📥 Download in the top-right corner to save a PDF report of your entire reconciliation analysis.

🧠 How Matching Works

Precifio uses a scoring engine that compares documents across multiple signals. Each signal has a weight, and the final score is a weighted average.

Match Types

Type Score What It Means
Exact 90–100% All key fields align perfectly. High confidence automatic match.
Strong 75–89% Most fields match well. Likely correct but worth a quick glance.
Partial Sum 80–89% Multiple Side B items add up to one Side A item (e.g., partial payments).
Split 75–84% Multiple Side A items add up to one Side B item (e.g., combined invoice).
Fuzzy 60–74% Some fields match but others are off. Flagged for human review.
Unmatched — No suitable match found on the other side.

Comparison Strategies

Strategy Best For Example
Exact with Tolerance Numbers, amounts, quantities $5,965.94 matches $5,965.94 (or within 1%)
Date Proximity Dates that may differ slightly Invoice July 28 matches payment August 2 (within 7 days)
Normalized Exact Reference numbers, IDs INV-20491 matches INV20491 (punctuation stripped)
Fuzzy Text Names, descriptions, vendors "ABC Trading Ltd" matches "ABC Trading Limited"

⚙️ Understanding Match Rules

Match rules tell Precifio which fields to compare and how to compare them. When you first run reconciliation, Precifio auto-detects rules by looking for field names that appear on both sides.

Example: Auto-Detected Rules

Side A (Invoice)
vendor_name
invoice_date
total_due
currency
↔
Side B (Purchase Order)
vendor_name
po_date
total
currency

Precifio detects: vendor_name ↔ vendor_name (fuzzy), total_due ↔ total (exact), currency ↔ currency (exact). You can override any of these.

💡 Pro Tip: If your CSV uses different column names (e.g., "Amount" vs "Total"), you can manually map them in the Match Rules screen. You don't need to rename your files.

📊 Sum Matching (Partial Payments & Combined Transactions)

Sometimes one invoice is paid in multiple installments. Or multiple invoices are paid with one lump sum. Precifio handles both.

Example: Partial Payment Match

Side A (Invoice)
Invoice #001
Total: ₦1,000,000
=
Side B (Payments)
Payment 1: ₦600,000
Payment 2: ₦400,000

Precifio detects that ₦600,000 + ₦400,000 = ₦1,000,000 and marks this as a Partial Sum match.

To enable sum matching, go to ⚙ Match Rules and turn on "Enable Sum Matching". Then specify which field contains the amount on each side.

✅ Tips for Best Results

  1. Use consistent date formats. ISO format (YYYY-MM-DD) works best, but most common formats are supported.
  2. Include reference numbers. Invoice numbers, PO numbers, and transaction IDs dramatically improve match accuracy.
  3. Clean your CSV before upload. Remove empty rows, header repeats, and summary totals that aren't actual line items.
  4. Name your fields clearly. "amount", "total", "payment" are all recognized as numeric. "ref", "reference", "invoice_number" are recognized as identifiers.
  5. Start with a small batch. Test with 10–20 documents first to verify your match rules before running on hundreds.
  6. Review the "Review" tab carefully. These are the matches Precifio is unsure about. A quick human check prevents errors.
  7. Use workspaces for time periods. Create one workspace per month or per project. Don't dump everything into one giant workspace.
  • You can remove individual documents. Hover over any document in Side A or Side B and click the ✕ to remove it if you uploaded the wrong file.

    🔐 Zero Retention by Default

    When you use Precifio Extract without Reconciliation, your documents and extracted data are purged immediately after processing. We store nothing.

    Reconciliation requires us to temporarily retain your structured data (the text and numbers our AI extracts) so we can compare documents over time.

    What we never store: Your original PDFs, images, scans, or files.

    Your control: Delete all reconciliation data instantly from your Settings — no support ticket required.

    What we will never do: Use your data to train AI models, sell it, or share it with third parties.

    ❓ Troubleshooting

    "No matches found"

    • Make sure both Side A and Side B have data. Check the tabs to confirm.
    • Review your Match Rules. Are the correct fields mapped?
    • Check if your amount fields are actually numeric (not text like "$1,000" — though Precifio handles most formats).

    "Too many unmatched items"

    • Increase the date tolerance in Match Rules (e.g., from 7 days to 14 days).
    • Increase the amount tolerance percentage slightly.
    • Enable Fuzzy Matching if it's turned off.
    • Check if your CSV has summary rows or totals that shouldn't be treated as individual documents.

    "Wrong documents are matching"

    • Add more match rules with higher weights. Reference numbers (invoice #, PO #) are the strongest signals.
    • Reduce the amount tolerance if amounts are matching incorrectly.
    • Check the "Review" tab — these are the ambiguous matches that need your attention.

    "I can't delete my data"

    • Open any workspace and click the ⚙ Settings button in the top-right. From there you can manage Field Aliases, export your settings, or click Delete All Data to permanently erase everything.
    • This is permanent and instant. There is no recovery.
