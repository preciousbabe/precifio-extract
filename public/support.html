Frequently Asked Questions

How do credits work?
Each document extraction costs 1 credit. Guest users get 1 free extraction. Signed-up users receive 10 complimentary credits. Additional credits can be purchased in-app at any time.
What document formats are supported?
We support PDF, JPG, PNG, and JPEG. More formats are coming soon. For best results, ensure your documents are clear and well-lit.
Is my data kept after extraction?
No. We operate a strict zero data-retention policy. Your documents and extracted data are held only for the duration of your active session and are permanently deleted once you export or close the app.
Can I export to Xero or QuickBooks?
Yes. Connect your Xero or QuickBooks account from the export settings panel, and extracted data can be pushed directly to your connected accounting platform.
I didn't receive my credits after purchase. What should I do?
First, refresh the page. If credits still don't appear, check your email for a receipt and contact us at support@precifio.app with your transaction ID.
How do I delete my account?
Send a request to support@precifio.app from your registered email address. We'll process the deletion within 48 hours and confirm once complete.
